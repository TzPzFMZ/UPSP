# Three-Step Round

Each UPSP Base round has three phases:

- `setup`: read context, suggest mounts, confirm the round type, and establish safe entry points.
- `reaction`: produce responses, call tools, operate work containers, and submit provider-native terminal forms.
- `cleanup`: organize training material, compact recent state, and settle required receipts.

The GUI should show the current phase, why it is waiting, which context and tools were involved, which receipts were produced, and whether cleanup or the single writer must act next. This is operational evidence, not decorative instrumentation.
