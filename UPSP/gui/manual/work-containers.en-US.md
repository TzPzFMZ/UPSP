# Work Containers

Work containers are UPSP's productive organs. They should not be flattened into one generic card list.

- **DC dialectic chains**: contradictions, thesis–antithesis–synthesis, and evidence chains.
- **EC event chains**: timelines, causal relations, and evidence replay.
- **PRJ project containers**: phases, milestones, acceptance, and risk.
- **SKL skill containers**: skill matrices, proficiency, and reflection logs.

A container page may occupy the central stage while the conversation moves into the remaining right-side space instead of compressing the work surface.
